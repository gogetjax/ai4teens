# Prompt: Verify my parts (text AI)

Use this in Phase 1, *after* you have drafted your own parts list. It checks your
work without doing it for you.

```
I'm making a labeled blow-out diagram of [YOUR OBJECT]. Here is the parts list I
came up with on my own, with where each part sits and what I think it does:

[PASTE YOUR DRAFT SPEC: each part, its position, its function]

Don't rewrite my list. Instead:
1. Tell me which parts I got wrong or placed in the wrong spot.
2. Tell me if I'm missing any important part a good diagram should show.
3. For anything I got wrong, explain why, but let me fix the wording myself.
Then ask me which part I'm least sure I could explain out loud, so we can go
deeper on that one.
```

Why it works: you do the decomposition first, so the struggle to name the parts
is yours. The AI only closes the gaps you could not close, which is exactly the
help you want and nothing more.
