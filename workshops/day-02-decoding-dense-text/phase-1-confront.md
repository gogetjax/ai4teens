# Phase 1 — Confront the passage (no AI yet)

Read your passage twice, slowly. Then, *before touching any AI*, fill in your
decode log:

1. **Circle every word or phrase you can't confidently define.** List them.
2. **Find the one sentence you understand least.** Copy it out.
3. **Try to say what the whole passage is about in one sentence.** If you can't,
   write "I can't yet, because ______."

That last line is the point. You're not failing — you're locating the work. A good
decoder doesn't pretend to understand; they pin down exactly what's in the way.

Don't move on until you've named at least three *specific* blockers. "It's all
confusing" is not a blocker. "I don't know what *intractable* means" is.
