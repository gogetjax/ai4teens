# Phase 3 — Rebuild it and present

Close the AI. Open your present-back worksheet.

1. **Explain the passage in ~60 seconds, in your own words.** Write it from
   memory, not by copying the AI.
2. **Pick one analogy and name where it breaks.** ("It's like ___, but that's
   wrong about ___.")
3. **Translate the original.** Take the one sentence you understood least in
   Phase 1 and rewrite it in plain English. This is your proof.
4. **Predict three questions** a sharp classmate might ask, and write your answers.

Then run `prompts/quiz-me-back.md`: paste your own-words explanation and let the
AI poke holes and quiz you. Fix what you got wrong — but keep the words yours.

Present to the group. Expect questions. If you can answer them without your notes,
you've actually learned it.
