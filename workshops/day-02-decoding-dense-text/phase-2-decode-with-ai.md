# Phase 2 — Decode with AI (use the ladder)

Now use the prompts in `prompts/`, roughly in this order. Don't ask the AI to
"explain the passage." Ask it to remove your *specific* blockers.

1. **Define the terms** (`prompts/define-the-terms.md`) — clear your unknown-word
   list first.
2. **Unpack the worst sentence** (`prompts/unpack-a-sentence.md`) — the one you
   copied in Phase 1.
3. **Build from fundamentals** (`prompts/build-from-fundamentals.md`) — find out
   what you needed to know *before* this passage, and learn that first.
4. **Explain at my level** (`prompts/explain-at-my-level.md`) — only after the
   pieces, get the whole thing retold at your level.
5. **Get an analogy and find its seams** (`prompts/analogy-and-where-it-breaks.md`).

Rule: every time the AI explains something, look away from the screen and say it
back to yourself out loud. If you can't, you didn't get it yet — ask a follow-up.
The goal is not a clean transcript; it's a head that no longer needs the
transcript.

Fill in your decode log as you go.
