# Prompt: Explain at my level

Use this *after* the pieces, to get the whole picture.

```
Explain this passage three times, each at a different level:
1. to a curious 10-year-old,
2. to a high-schooler who hasn't taken this subject,
3. the way a textbook would, but with every piece of jargon defined right where
   it appears.
Use a genuinely different angle each time — not the same sentences reworded.
Afterward, ask me one question to find out which version actually landed for me.

Passage:
[PASTE THE PASSAGE]
```

Why it works: three levels let you find the rung you can actually stand on, then
climb.
