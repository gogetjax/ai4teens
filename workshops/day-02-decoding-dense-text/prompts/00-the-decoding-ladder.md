# The Decoding Ladder

The goal of every prompt here is the same: get the AI to *help you understand* so
that **you** can re-explain it — not to get the AI to write the explanation for
you. If your final presentation is just the AI's words, you've learned nothing and
you'll freeze on the first question.

Use these roughly in order:
1. `define-the-terms.md` — kill the unknown words first.
2. `unpack-a-sentence.md` — for the single worst sentence.
3. `build-from-fundamentals.md` — learn what you needed to know *before* the passage.
4. `explain-at-my-level.md` — now get the whole thing, retold at your level.
5. `analogy-and-where-it-breaks.md` — get an analogy and learn its limits.
6. `quiz-me-back.md` — prove you've got it; let the AI poke holes.

**The golden rule:** after every AI answer, look away and say it back out loud. If
you can't, ask one more follow-up. The transcript isn't the point — the head that
no longer needs it is.

Notice the shape of every prompt below: it tells the AI to explain in *pieces*, to
avoid new jargon, and to **end by asking you something**. That last part keeps you
doing the thinking.
