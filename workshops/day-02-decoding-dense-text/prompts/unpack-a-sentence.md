# Prompt: Unpack one sentence

Use this on the single sentence you understood least.

```
Here is one sentence I can't parse:

"[PASTE THE SENTENCE]"

Rewrite it in plain English a 14-year-old could follow. Then break it into its
parts and tell me what job each part is doing. Don't add any information that
isn't already in the sentence. When you're done, ask me to say it back in my own
words so you can check whether I actually got it.
```

Why it works: "tell me the job each part is doing" turns a wall of words into a few
moving pieces.
