# Prompt: Analogy — and where it breaks

Use this to get an analogy you can present with honestly.

```
Give me two or three different analogies for the core idea in this passage. For
EACH analogy, immediately tell me where it breaks down — what part of the real
idea the analogy gets wrong or leaves out. I'm going to use an analogy when I
present, so I need to know its limits. Don't pick for me; let me choose.

Passage:
[PASTE THE PASSAGE]
```

Why it works: an analogy you can break on purpose proves you understand the real
thing, not just the comparison.
