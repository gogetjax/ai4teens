# Prompt: Quiz me back

Use this last, once you've written your own-words explanation. This is the test.

```
I'm about to present this to my class. Here is my explanation, in my own words:

"[PASTE YOUR OWN EXPLANATION]"

Don't rewrite it for me. Instead:
1. Point out anything I got wrong or said too vaguely.
2. Ask me three questions a sharp classmate might ask.
3. Wait for my answers before telling me whether they're right — don't give the
   answers up front.
```

Why it works: if you can survive the quiz without the AI feeding you lines, you
actually know it.
