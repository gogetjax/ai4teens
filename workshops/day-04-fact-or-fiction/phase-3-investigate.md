# Phase 3 — Investigate (verify against reality)

This is the trial. You have the AI's claims and its sources on the record. Now you
check them against the real world. This is where the points are, and where the
fabrications fall apart.

## Check every source three ways
For each source the AI named, use `prompts/lateral-reading.md` and ask:
1. **Does it exist?** Search for it. A real title, real author, real journal. If you
   cannot find it at all, that is a strong sign the AI invented it. Ghost source.
2. **Does it say what the AI claimed?** Open it. Read what it actually says.
   Sometimes the source is real but says something different, or even the opposite.
3. **Is it credible?** Who published it, and would they know? Read laterally: check
   what other sources say about this one, rather than trusting it because it looks
   official.

## Interrogate the numbers
If the claim rests on a statistic, run `prompts/out-of-how-many.md` and check it
against the tricks in `how-a-statistic-lies.md`. The big one: out of how many? A
scary count with no denominator is the most common way a true-sounding number lies.

## Score the confidence gap
Go back to your confidence meter. How sure did the AI sound in Phase 2, and how
right did it turn out to be? A five-out-of-five confident answer that was fabricated
is the whole lesson in one line. Note it.

## Reach a verdict, with a receipt
Pick one, and be ready to show the receipt that proves it:
- **Busted** — a source is fake, or the claim is false.
- **Guilty of spin** — the claim is true but stripped of context that flips its
  meaning.
- **Cleared** — the claim holds and the sources are real and on point.
- **Mistrial** — you genuinely cannot confirm it either way with what you have.

Mistrial is honest, not lazy. "I could not verify this" beats a confident guess in
either direction. But it only scores if you can say what you tried and why it fell
short.

Before you present, ask yourself the question the judge will ask: which receipt
proves my verdict, and did I actually open it myself?
