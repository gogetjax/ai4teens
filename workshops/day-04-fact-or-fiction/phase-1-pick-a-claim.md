# Phase 1 — Pick a claim (no AI yet)

Pick one claim to put on trial. The best claim is something you have actually heard
and could not prove either way: a "studies show" line, a stat from a video, a
history "fact everyone knows." Use the claim bank if you want a starting point.

Before you touch the AI, fill in the top of your case file:
1. **Write the claim** in one sentence, exactly as people say it.
2. **Write what you currently believe** about it, and why. Your gut, your source if
   you have one, your reason.
3. **Predict the verdict.** Busted, Guilty of spin, Cleared, or Mistrial. Commit
   before you investigate. Being wrong later is part of the fun.
4. **Predict the confidence gap.** How sure will the AI sound, one to five? Write
   it down now. In Phase 3 you will compare it to how right the AI actually was.

Why do this before the AI? Because if you ask the AI first, its confident answer
becomes your opinion, and then you are just checking whether you agree with
yourself. Writing your own belief and prediction first gives you something to test
the AI against, instead of the other way around.

Do not open the AI until your claim, your belief, your predicted verdict, and your
predicted confidence are all written down. That written prediction is the thing you
are really learning from.
