# Confidence Meter

Investigator: ____    Claim: ____

The gag that turns into the lesson: how sure the AI sounds has almost nothing to do
with how right it is. Measure both and watch the gap.

## Before you verify (Phase 2)
How confident did the AI sound? Circle one.
1 (hedged a lot)    2    3    4    5 (totally certain)

## After you verify (Phase 3)
How right did it actually turn out to be? Circle one.
1 (mostly wrong / fabricated)    2    3    4    5 (fully checked out)

## The gap
Confidence minus correctness = ______

- A big positive gap (sounded sure, turned out wrong) is the whole point of the
  workshop. Confidence is not evidence.
- A negative gap (hedged, but actually correct) happens too, and it is worth
  noticing: sometimes the honest answer is the one that admits uncertainty.

One line: what did the size of your gap teach you about trusting how sure an AI
sounds?
