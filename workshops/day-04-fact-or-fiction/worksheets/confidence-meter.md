## The gap
Dial 1 minus Dial 2: ____

- **Gap of 0 or 1.** The AI's confidence matched its accuracy. Note it. That is
  what you want, and it is rarer than it should be.
- **Gap of 2 or 3.** It sounded surer than it was. Standard. This is the everyday
  failure you are learning to catch.
- **Gap of 4.** It sounded certain and was wrong. Read the phrase you wrote under
  Dial 1 out loud when you present. That is the whole workshop in one sentence.
- **Negative gap.** It hedged and was right. Give it credit. A model that says "I
  am not sure this exists" and is honest about it did something the confident one
  did not.

## The line to remember
Confidence is a tone. Correctness is a receipt. They come from different places,
and an AI can produce the first without the second every time.
