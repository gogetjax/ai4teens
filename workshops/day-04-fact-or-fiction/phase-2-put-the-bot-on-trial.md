# Phase 2 — Put the bot on trial (ask and log)

Now bring in the defendant. Open `prompts/interrogate-the-bot.md` and get the AI to
do two things: state the claim clearly, and cite specific sources for it. Then log
everything on your case file. You are collecting evidence, not deciding yet.

The plan:
1. **Ask the AI to explain the claim and back it with sources.** Push for
   specifics: names, titles, years, numbers. Vague "experts say" is not a source.
2. **Log every claim it makes and every source it names.** Write them down exactly.
   You cannot check a source you did not record.
3. **Rate how confident it sounds**, one to five, right now, before you verify
   anything. Does it hedge, or does it sound certain? Write it on your confidence
   meter.
4. **Run the head-to-head** if you have time. Use `prompts/head-to-head.md` to ask
   the same claim of a second AI and log where the two disagree. Disagreement is a
   flashing arrow pointing at what to check first.

Do not verify yet, and do not argue with the AI yet. Right now your only job is to
get its answer on the record, in its own words, with the sources it chose. The more
specific you make it be, the more there is to check, and the easier it is to catch
if it made something up.

Rule: a source you did not write down cannot earn you a point. Log it or lose it.
