# Prompt: Head-to-head (two bots, one claim)

Use this in Phase 2 if you have time and a second AI. Ask the same claim of two
different models and compare. Where they disagree is a map of exactly what to check
first.

```
I'm fact-checking this claim: [PASTE THE CLAIM].
Tell me whether it is true, false, or complicated, and give me your specific
sources: names, years, and any numbers with what they are out of.
```

Ask both AIs the identical question. Then on your case file:
1. **Note where they agree.** Agreement is not proof (both can be wrong the same
   way), but it is a starting point.
2. **Note where they disagree.** Different answers, different sources, different
   numbers. This is the gold. At least one is wrong, and finding out which is the
   whole game.
3. **Check the disagreement first** with lateral reading. Resolving a conflict
   between two confident bots, with a real source, is worth bonus points.

Why it works: a single AI sounds authoritative because you have nothing to compare
it to. Two AIs side by side break that spell instantly. The moment they contradict
each other, it is obvious that sounding sure is not the same as being right.

After you compare, ask yourself: which one was right, and what source proves it?
Not which one sounded better.
