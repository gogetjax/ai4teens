# Prompt: Head-to-head (Phase 2, optional)

Ask a second AI the exact same question you asked the first. Paste the same
interrogation prompt, word for word, into a different chatbot. Then compare the
two answers side by side on your case file.

```
I am investigating this claim: "[YOUR CLAIM, EXACTLY AS PEOPLE SAY IT]"

Do three things:
1. State the claim clearly in one sentence, in the strongest form people actually
   use.
2. Explain why people believe it and what evidence supports it.
3. Cite at least three specific sources for that evidence. For each one give the
   author or organization, the title, the year, and where it was published. If a
   source contains a specific number, quote the number and say what it is out of.

Be as specific as you can. Do not say "studies show" or "experts agree" without
naming the study or the expert. If you are not certain a source is real, say so
next to that source.
```

## What to look for
- **Same source, both bots.** Slightly more likely to be real, but not proof.
  Two AIs can share the same wrong memory. Still verify.
- **Different sources.** Normal. Check both lists.
- **Same source, different details.** A title that matches but a year or author
  that does not is a flashing arrow. One of them is wrong, and possibly both.
- **Different numbers for the same claim.** Somebody is missing a denominator or a
  definition. Start `out-of-how-many.md` there.
- **One hedges, one does not.** Note which one sounded surer. Check whether the
  sure one was right. This is the confidence gap in a single comparison.

Why it works: a disagreement between two AIs tells you where to look first. It
does not tell you who is right. Only a receipt does that. Do not settle a
disagreement by asking a third AI to break the tie.
