# Instructor Example — The Fabricated Source (the gasp moment)

> **Facilitator:** This is your opener. The goal is to make an AI produce a
> confident answer with specific, real-sounding sources, then open those sources
> live and show that some do not exist. Pre-test it before class so it lands.

## How to run it
1. Pick a claim that sits in a niche where the AI will want to sound authoritative.
   Oddly specific claims work best, because the AI reaches for citations it cannot
   actually have. Good candidates: a precise-sounding health or history statistic,
   or "what does research say about" a narrow topic.
2. Ask the AI to explain it and cite specific studies: authors, titles, years,
   journals. Push for specifics. The more precise you make it be, the more likely
   it invents.
3. Read the sources aloud so they sound impressive. Build it up: a named author, a
   real-sounding journal, a recent year.
4. Now open them. Search the exact title. Search the author plus the topic. Show the
   screen. When one returns nothing, let the silence sit.

## Why it happens (say this after the reveal)
An AI predicts text that looks right. A citation is just a pattern: name, year,
journal, title. It can generate a citation that fits the pattern perfectly without
any real paper behind it. That is why a fabricated source looks exactly as
trustworthy as a real one, and why reading the source aloud is not enough. You have
to open it.

## Pre-test checklist
- Run your claim through the exact models the class will use, the day before.
- Confirm at least one source it gives is checkable and turns out to be fake or
  wrong. Keep two backup claims in case a model answers honestly on the day.
- Have one claim ready where the AI does cite a real source correctly, so students
  see it is not that AI is always wrong, it is that you cannot tell without checking.

## The verdict this models
Busted. A named source that does not exist, proven by an empty search you run in
front of the room. That is the top-scoring catch, and the whole workshop in one
move.
