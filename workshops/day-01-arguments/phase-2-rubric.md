# Counterargument Rubric

Use this to score a counterargument (yours, a classmate's, or the AI's). For each row, pick
the level that fits best.

| Criterion | 1 — Emerging | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Strong |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|----------------|------------|
| Targets real weaknesses | Mostly just disagrees; names no weak points | Hits one real weakness | Hits most real weaknesses | Hits the real weaknesses precisely and says why each is weak |
| Handles the strongest point | Ignores it | Mentions but dodges it | Addresses it fairly | Addresses it and shows why the original still doesn't hold |
| No straw man | Attacks a version no one argued | Slightly distorts the original | Represents the original fairly | Represents the original at its strongest, then counters |
| Sound reasoning | Relies on assertions or its own fallacies | Some support, some shaky moves | Mostly sound reasoning | Clear, fallacy-free reasoning with realistic support |
| Beats the argument, not the arguer | Attacks the person or their motives | Some personal jabs | Stays on the argument | Entirely focused on the ideas |

**Quick read:**
- 17-20 — a genuinely strong counterargument
- 12-16 — solid, with a clear weak spot to fix
- below 12 — disagrees more than it counters; revise

The two most common ways to lose points: dodging the strongest point, or "winning" against
a straw man.
