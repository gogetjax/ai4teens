# Day 1 — Arguments & Counterarguments

**Goal:** Students learn to take apart someone else's argument, then use AI to build
and stress-test a counterargument — understanding *why* a counterargument works, not
just generating one.

## Learning objectives
By the end, students can:
- Identify the claim, reasons, evidence, and hidden assumptions in an argument
- Judge the strengths and weaknesses of an argument
- Use AI as a thinking partner to analyze an argument (Phase 1)
- Use AI to generate a counterargument and evaluate why it's effective (Phase 2)

## Agenda
1. Warm-up: what makes something an *argument* vs. just an opinion? (10 min)
2. Read the example argument together (5 min)
3. Phase 1 — break it down by hand, then with AI (see `phase-1-analysis.md`)
4. Phase 2 — build and evaluate a counterargument (see `phase-2-counterargument.md`)
5. Debrief: where did AI help, and where did it try to do your thinking for you?

## Facilitator note on AI use
The prompts are written to make the AI *show its reasoning* and *hand judgment back to
the student*. Have students attempt each breakdown on their own first, then compare with
the AI. The gap between the two is the lesson.
