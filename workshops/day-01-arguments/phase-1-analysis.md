# Phase 1 — Analyzing the Argument

## What students produce
A six-part breakdown of Jordan's argument:

1. Main claim — what is Jordan trying to convince people of? (one sentence)
2. Reasons — each reason given, listed separately
3. Evidence — facts/examples used, each rated strong / okay / weak (with why)
4. Hidden assumptions — what Jordan takes for granted without saying it
5. Strengths — what's actually convincing, and why
6. Weaknesses — shaky reasoning, weak evidence, or logical fallacies (named + explained)

## How to run it
1. Students fill in `worksheets/student-worksheet.md` on their own first — no AI.
2. Then they run the prompt in `prompts/phase-1-analyze.md`, pasting in the argument.
3. They compare: What did the AI catch that they missed? What did they catch that the
   AI stated weakly or got wrong?
4. Discuss: the AI is a second reader, not the answer key.
